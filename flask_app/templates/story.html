CHAPTER 03

🔋 The Battery Chemistry Race

Battery chemistry determines the holy trinity of electric mobility: range, cost, and lifecycle safety.

NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt) remains the industry workhorse powering 60% of models due to balanced energy density. LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) is winning cost-sensitive markets with thermal resilience, while Solid-State (e.g., NIO ET5) sets new range records crossing 1,000 km on a single charge.

1,000 km Solid-State Peak
